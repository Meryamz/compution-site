Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Points, unlocks and game progress stay on your device. There is no sign-in and no cloud save. Online play uses a player identifier and preset public profile. Usage analytics can be switched off at any time. Purchases, crash reporting and advertising use the services explained below.

About this policy

Umpteen is made by Compution Ltd. This policy covers the iOS and Android app, including the online and Plus features in version 5.0. Earlier versions may not include all of these features. Solo games can be played offline; online features, advertising and store purchases use internet services.

Versions 2.5 and earlier have no online accounts, leaderboards, analytics or crash reporting. Their Play Together finds nearby phones on the same Wi-Fi network and sends game moves and the chosen player name (and, in Doodle Guess, drawings and typed guesses) directly between those phones; none of it passes through our servers. Advertising in those versions works as described below; their Remove Ads purchase was handled by Apple or Google directly, without RevenueCat.

Progress on your device

Scores, settings, favourites, points, earned game unlocks and game progress are stored on your device. Umpteen 5.0 has no sign-in and no cloud save, so we do not hold a copy. Your phone’s own backup (Android backup, or an iCloud or computer backup) may include this saved data in your Google or Apple account and restore it to a new phone; without such a backup, removing the app or its data, or moving to another device, loses them. A future version may offer optional cloud saving; we will update this policy before it does. The app does not send notifications, request GPS location, or access your contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Online play and leaderboards

When you use online features, including Ranks, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account identifier. Firebase Realtime Database stores your nickname and badge (assigned from presets, which you can change), friend code, friend and invitation records, short-lived matchmaking, room and match-connection records, match data and leaderboard entries (weekly points totals and game scores). Other players can see leaderboard entries (nickname, badge and score) and what is needed for a match; friends can also see your profile. The app does not require an email address for online play and has no free-text player chat. We do not use your Apple Account, Game Center or Google Play Games name as your public player name.

Firebase App Check helps validate genuine app requests. Service providers also process network and device information needed to operate and secure their services. We use these records to provide online play, rankings, reliability and abuse prevention. Online records are kept until removed by feature cleanup or a deletion request; they do not all expire automatically after a fixed period.

Optional usage analytics

In Umpteen 5.0, usage analytics helps us see which games are played and what to improve. In the UK (when your phone’s region is the UK and it is set to UK time), and in countries where no consent is required, it is on for players who tell us they are 18 or older, unless you turn off Share anonymous usage in Settings → Privacy. It stays off unless a grown-up turns it on if you tell us you are under 18 or have not answered the age question, in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, and wherever Google’s consent framework says consent is needed. When on, Google Analytics for Firebase receives events such as which game was opened, round outcomes and whether help was used, which tab or tutorial page was viewed, ad and reward-button outcomes, keep-going and points-double choices, shop, unlock and subscription interactions, which help page was opened, how long a game or round lasted and the difficulty chosen, points earned and spent, search use (how many characters and results, never the words typed), favourites and filters, shares, daily challenges, sound and commentary settings, online match starts and ends, friend and invite outcomes, and online connection failures. It also keeps your plan (free, Plus or legacy Remove Ads) and the first game you played as profile properties. Firebase adds automatic events such as first open, session start, engagement time, app removal, in-app purchases and ad impressions. Nothing is sent before the app has decided whether analytics is on. Button location means a screen or placement in the app, not your physical location.

These events use game identifiers and controlled categories rather than player names, friend codes or raw error messages. The analytics service processes its own app-instance and device information. Advertising storage and personalisation signals are disabled in our usage-analytics configuration. You can turn usage analytics off at any time; this stops future collection and resets the local analytics identifier. It does not automatically erase reports already uploaded.

Crash diagnostics

Release builds use Firebase Crashlytics to report errors and stack traces so we can fix crashes. The service also processes diagnostic information such as app and operating-system versions and installation identifiers. Our crash-reporting integration does not attach player names, custom user IDs or custom gameplay logs. If you enable usage analytics, Firebase may include recent analytics events as diagnostic breadcrumbs to help identify what happened before an error. Crash diagnostics are separate from the usage-analytics setting; there is no in-app switch for them, and debug and test builds send none.

Purchases and Umpteen Plus

Apple or Google handles checkout and payment details. RevenueCat processes store purchase and subscription records, receipts or purchase tokens, product and entitlement status and an anonymous customer identifier so we can unlock Plus, restore purchases and understand subscription performance. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Purchase records may be kept to provide ongoing access, restore transactions, prevent fraud and meet legal or accounting requirements. Deleting an online player profile does not cancel a store subscription or automatically delete store or RevenueCat purchase records. Contact us for help with purchase-data requests. Manage cancellation through the store where you subscribed.

Advertising and your choices

Google AdMob provides interstitial ads at eligible breaks, such as between rounds or at half time, and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice: for example, to double the points from a round or, in some games, to keep going once after losing a run. Its SDK may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and protect advertising. We request consent through Google’s consent flow where applicable and request iOS tracking permission where required. Declining tracking does not prevent solo play. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, and there are no banner or app-open ads. If you tell us you are under 13, ads are child-directed; if you are 13 to 17, or have not answered, ads are never personalised, whatever the consent choice. Ad availability depends on consent, eligibility and network conditions.

Settings → Ads & privacy reopens Google’s privacy choices where available. Your phone settings also provide advertising and tracking controls. Active Plus and restored permanent Remove Ads ownership suppress ads; eligible rewards use an ad-free claim instead. To report an ad, contact hello@compution.co.uk with what appeared, roughly when, and your device type.

Children

Umpteen is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over, not the Kids Category. When the app first opens, it asks how old you are, with three answers: under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older. Under 13, stranger matchmaking is off and ads are child-directed. Under 18, usage analytics stays off unless a grown-up turns it on, and ads are never personalised. If the question is closed without an answer, the app treats you as under 18 and asks again next time, and before online play. Players under 13 can still play friends by code, and their preset nickname, badge and scores can still appear on leaderboards. The answer can be changed in Settings after a grown-up check. The age band is not sent to us or to usage analytics; it is kept with the app’s data on the device (and in your phone’s own backup, if any). It is not a date of birth. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed.

Sharing, links and clipboard

Share buttons open your phone’s share sheet, after a grown-up check, with a result picture or a short message such as a game or friend code and a link to our website. You choose where it goes; we do not receive it. Copy buttons put a code on your clipboard, and the app reads the clipboard only when you tap a paste button, such as to load a friend’s ghost code in Ridge Rider. Help, policy and subscription links open in an in-app browser view or your browser, under that website’s own policy.

Providers, retention and international processing

We use Google Firebase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat and the Apple or Google store for the purposes described above. Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable data-protection arrangements. As verified on 31 October 2026, Analytics event data retention is set to 2 months and user data retention to 14 months, with the user-data period reset by new activity. These controls do not govern most aggregated reports. We retain other information for the relevant service, security, support or legal purpose; retention differs between online records, analytics, diagnostics and transaction records. Aggregated reports and records required for legal or fraud-prevention purposes may remain after an individual request.

More information: Firebase privacy and security, Google Privacy Policy, RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Access, deletion and privacy requests

On the Online tab, tap your player card and choose Forget me online to remove your online profile, friend code, friendships, invitations, leaderboard entries and any room or matchmaking records you own, before deleting the anonymous Firebase account. Local game progress is preserved. If removal fails, the app keeps the identity available so you can retry. Returning to online features as a guest can create a new anonymous profile.

You can also request help through our data-deletion page or email hello@compution.co.uk. We may need information to locate the relevant anonymous records and verify your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. UK users can raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Contact and changes

Compution Ltd is responsible for the app’s handling of your information. Email hello@compution.co.uk for privacy questions. Support emails include the contact details and information you choose to send. We update this page when practices change and revise the date above.
