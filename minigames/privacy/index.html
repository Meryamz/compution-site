Mini Fun Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

The short version: Mini Fun Games has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The app shows occasional ads served by Google AdMob, and Google's advertising SDK collects some device data to serve and measure those ads — with your permission where the law requires it.

Overview

This privacy policy explains how Mini Fun Games ("the app"), made by Compution Ltd, handles your information. It applies to both the iOS and Android versions of the app. Mini Fun Games may appear on your home screen as "Mini Games".

All of the games work entirely offline. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to, and no content you create in the app ever leaves your device.

What We Don't Collect

  • The app does not require an account or registration, and never asks for your name, email address, or any contact details.
  • We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.
  • Compution Ltd does not itself collect, store, or transmit any personal data from the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves things like your best scores, win streaks, and settings (for example, sound preferences) so they are there the next time you play. This data is stored locally on your device only, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

Mini Fun Games is free and is supported by occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect information from your device, including:

  • A device advertising identifier (the advertising ID on Android, or the IDFA on iOS — only if you allow tracking).
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate general location.
  • General device information, such as the model and operating system version.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were viewed or tapped.

This data is collected by Google, not by us — we never see it and have no access to it. Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and its advertising technologies page.

Your Choices and Consent

On iOS

Tracking on iOS is controlled by Apple's App Tracking Transparency system: the app cannot access your device's advertising identifier unless you have explicitly allowed tracking. Without that permission you simply see non-personalised ads, and the games play exactly the same. You can review tracking permissions at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the UK, EU, and other regions with consent laws

Where the law requires consent, ads are only personalised if you agree through Google's consent flow. If you have not consented, you will see non-personalised ads instead, and the games are unaffected either way.

On Android

You can opt out of ad personalisation, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device's settings under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path can vary by device).

Children

The app is made for a general audience. It has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, and no way to enter personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads shown in the app are served by Google and are subject to Google's advertising policies.

Your Rights

Because Compution Ltd does not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. For data collected by Google to serve ads, the Google Privacy Policy linked above explains the controls and rights available to you. If you are in the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to This Policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this policy and revise the "last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at hello@compution.co.uk.
