Quick answers for Umpteen 5.0

What can I spend my points on?

Open Shop to unlock individual premium games with your points. Each card shows its price. Earned unlocks stay available on that device even if Plus ends. Guest points and earned unlocks are local progress, so removing app data or changing devices may lose them. Where Save my progress is available in Settings, link an account for free and confirm that the save completes before removing the app. Cricket Smash is a free premium game for everyone.

What does Plus include?

Umpteen Plus unlocks all premium games and removes ads while your subscription is active. Choose monthly or yearly billing in the app; your store shows the price and renewal terms before you confirm. An earned game unlock stays available when Plus ends.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Use Restore purchases in the app with the store account that bought the subscription, on the same platform. Existing permanent Remove Ads purchases can also be restored. Restore does not transfer local points or earned game unlocks. Manage or cancel billing in your Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

Why is a reward video unavailable?

Video availability depends on your connection, advertising choices and the ad provider. Rewards also have a cooldown and daily allowance, shown in the app. A reward is added only when the provider confirms you earned it. Eligible ad-free players see a claim button instead. If a completed video did not reward you, tell us the screen, approximate time and device model.

Online play or rankings will not connect

Check your connection and try again from the online screen. You can keep playing solo games while online features are unavailable. Tell us which game or screen failed and what the message said. Never send your password.

Can I recover points after reinstalling?

Where Settings offers Save my progress, choose Apple or Google sign-in and upload your progress before removing the app. After reinstalling, choose Restore progress and use the same account. Review the saved progress before replacing the current device progress. Only uploaded progress can be recovered; guest progress and unsaved offline changes may be lost. Reconnect before spending points on a new premium game so the shared balance can be confirmed. Store purchases use the separate Restore Purchases option.

How do I delete my saved-progress account?

Where this feature is available, open Settings → Your saved progress → Delete account. Confirm and sign in again to verify ownership. This removes the private save, associated owned online records and Firebase account and resets local progress. If interrupted, use Finish deleting when connected. Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription. Our deletion help page also explains how to request assistance without the app.

How do I change my privacy and reminder choices?

In Settings, Usage privacy controls optional usage analytics, Ads & privacy opens available advertising choices, and Daily discovery controls optional play reminders. To remove your online profile and scores, use Forget me online in Play Together. See our privacy policy and deletion help for details.
