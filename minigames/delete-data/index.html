Data deletion

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Delete your Umpteen online profile

Open the Online tab in Umpteen, tap your player card, then choose Forget me online and confirm. This removes the anonymous online account and associated profile, friend-code, friend/invitation, leaderboard, room and matchmaking records. Local progress and earned unlocks stay on your phone. If the app cannot complete removal, reconnect and retry.

Points and progress on your phone

Umpteen 5.0 has no sign-in and no cloud save. Points, earned unlocks and game progress are stored on your device, and we hold no copy. Removing the app or its data deletes them from the phone; your phone’s own backup (Android backup, or an iCloud or computer backup) may keep a copy until you delete that backup. Removing the app does not cancel Plus; manage the subscription through Apple or Google.

Request deletion without the app

Email hello@compution.co.uk with the subject “Umpteen data deletion request”. Include your platform and, if available, your preset player name and friend code to help locate records. We verify ownership before acting on a deletion request. A public friend code alone does not prove ownership. If you no longer have the app, tell us; do not reinstall just to obtain a new profile.

This opens your email app; you must send the email yourself. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or identity documents unless we specifically explain why a further verification step is necessary.

Other records and subscriptions

Online deletion does not cancel Plus. Cancel through your Apple or Google store account to stop renewal. Purchase records needed for access restoration, fraud prevention or legal obligations may remain. Tell us if your request also concerns RevenueCat purchase records or support correspondence. Optional analytics can be disabled in Settings → Usage privacy; previously uploaded aggregated reports may remain. See the privacy policy for the data and services involved.
