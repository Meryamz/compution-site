Umpteen Plus

Plus gives access to all premium games offered in the app and removes ads while the subscription is active. Monthly and annual billing options are shown with the store’s localized price before checkout. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your store account. Check the store’s purchase screen for the billing period, renewal date, price and cancellation conditions. Deleting the app or an online profile does not cancel your subscription.

When Plus ends, subscription-only premium access ends. Games unlocked with earned points remain unlocked on that device. Points and earned unlocks are local progress; they are not store purchases and cannot be restored with Restore purchases. Removing app data or moving to another device may lose this progress. Cricket Smash is free for everyone. Historical permanent Remove Ads purchases remain restorable and remove ads but do not include Plus game access. Restore purchases using the purchasing store account on the same platform; shared iOS/Android subscription ownership is not currently offered.
