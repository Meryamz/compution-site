REPLATE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

The short version: REPLATE is built so that we cannot see your data, because it never leaves your device.

Overview

This privacy policy explains how REPLATE ("the app"), a workout planner and log made by Compution Ltd, handles your information when you use the app.

What We Collect

Nothing.

REPLATE has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and makes no network connections of its own. Your workouts, plans, streaks and bodyweight readings are stored in a single file on your device and nowhere else. We have no servers that could receive them.

What Leaves the App

A few actions open other apps on your device, at your request:

  • Website and the links to this policy and the terms open your browser.
  • Send feedback opens your email app with our address pre-filled.
  • Export and Backup hand a file you chose to export to the share sheet, so you decide where it goes.

In every case the other app's own privacy policy applies from that point. REPLATE sends nothing on its own.

Your Data, Your Device

Deleting the app deletes your data. Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google's device backup) may include the app's data in your personal backup, under your platform account and your platform's privacy policy. We never see it.

Purchases

REPLATE is free to download. One optional purchase, REPLATE Pro, unlocks extra analysis and tools; it is bought once and owned for good. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive no payment details and no personal data.

The app keeps a record of the unlock on your device so that Pro still works offline. That record is the only thing it stores about the purchase, and it is checked against Apple or Google again on each launch. Restore purchase re-reads that record from your App Store or Google Play account.

Children

REPLATE does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of REPLATE ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version ships, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised, and the change will be called out in the release notes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at hello@compution.co.uk.
